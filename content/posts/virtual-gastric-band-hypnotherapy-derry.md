## What Is a Virtual Gastric Band?

Gastric band surgery involves fitting a physical device around the upper portion of the stomach, reducing its capacity so that you feel full after eating far less than before. It works, but it comes with general anaesthetic, a recovery period, the risk of surgical complications, and a price tag that puts it out of reach for most people.

The Virtual Gastric Band is a clinical hypnotherapy technique that creates the same psychological outcome, without any of the physical intervention.

Using the power of the subconscious mind, the brain is guided to *believe* that the stomach has been reduced in size. The result is a genuine change in how quickly and how strongly fullness is registered. This is not through suggestion that you simply tell yourself to believe, but through a deep subconscious shift that changes how you experience eating at a neurological level.

## How Does the Mind Affect Physical Sensation?

This might sound implausible until you consider how powerfully the mind shapes physical experience all the time.

Think about the last time you felt nervous before an important event: the knot in the stomach, the loss of appetite. No food had changed. No surgery had been performed. The mind changed the physical sensation directly.

Or consider how people with certain phobias experience genuine nausea, sweating, and elevated heart rate in response to a photograph, an entirely non-threatening stimulus that the mind has labelled as dangerous. The physical response is completely real, generated entirely by a subconscious programme.

The Virtual Gastric Band works through the same mechanism, but in a constructive direction. The subconscious mind is guided to register fullness earlier and more strongly. The body follows that instruction, consistently, at every meal.

## What the Virtual Gastric Band Programme Involves

Unlike a standard hypnotherapy session for weight loss, the Virtual Gastric Band is typically delivered as a structured programme over three to four sessions. This allows the technique to be introduced progressively, with reinforcement built in as your mind and body begin to adjust.

A typical programme with Tracey includes:

**Session one: Assessment and preparation.** Tracey will explore your history with food, eating patterns, and any emotional drivers behind your weight. This shapes the entire programme. The first hypnotherapy session introduces deep relaxation and begins the mental preparation for change.

**Session two: The Virtual Gastric Band.** This is the central session. In a deep hypnotic state, the subconscious mind is guided through a detailed, sensory experience of the gastric band procedure. The mind does not distinguish clearly between a vividly imagined experience and a real one. That is the basis of the technique's effectiveness.

**Session three: Reinforcement and habits.** The physical change in appetite sensation is reinforced, and the emotional patterns around eating (comfort eating, boredom eating, reward eating) are addressed directly.

**Session four (optional): Consolidation.** For clients who want additional depth, a final session embeds the changes and provides a self-hypnosis technique to use independently going forward.

## What Results Can You Expect?

Results vary depending on the individual, their relationship with food, and how fully they engage with the programme. That said, clients typically report:

- A natural reduction in portion sizes, with satisfaction after noticeably less food
- Reduced cravings for high-calorie, processed, or sugary foods
- A change in eating pace, eating more slowly and stopping when genuinely full
- Reduced episodes of emotional eating or eating from habit rather than hunger
- Gradual, sustainable weight loss without the psychological strain of strict dieting

The weight loss produced by the Virtual Gastric Band tends to be slower than surgical options, typically one to two pounds per week, but it is far more sustainable, because it is driven by a genuine change in appetite and relationship with food rather than a mechanical restriction.

## Is It a Replacement for Surgery?

For most people considering weight loss surgery, the Virtual Gastric Band is a far more appropriate first step. It is safer, more affordable, more accessible, and addresses the psychological drivers of weight gain that surgery alone leaves completely untouched.

Surgery reduces the stomach's capacity. It does not address why someone uses food for comfort, reward, or control. These patterns will reassert themselves over time even in people who have had physical procedures.

Hypnotherapy addresses both the appetite and the psychology simultaneously.

It is worth noting that the Virtual Gastric Band is not a medical procedure and is not a replacement for medical advice. If you have a BMI that puts you in a clinical risk category, please discuss all options with your GP. Hypnotherapy can work effectively alongside medical guidance.
