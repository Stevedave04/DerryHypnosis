## Why There Is No Price List

Two people can walk in wanting the same thing and need completely different work.

One wants to stop smoking, has wanted to for a year, has a firm date in mind and no other complications. That is a single intensive session, and they leave a non-smoker.

The other wants to stop smoking too, but smokes when anxious, and the anxiety came from something that has never been addressed. Sell them the same single session and you have sold them the wrong thing. They need the anxiety worked on, or the smoking comes back the first bad week they have.

A fixed price list encourages a therapist to fit you to the package rather than the package to you. Quoting individually, after a conversation, means you are told what you actually need — including, sometimes, that you need less than you thought, or that hypnotherapy is not the right approach and something else is.

That conversation costs nothing, which is the point of the next section.

## The Real Question: How Many Sessions?

A single session fee tells you very little if you have no idea whether you are buying one or ten. This is the number that actually determines what you spend.

Honest ranges, based on how this work typically goes:

| What you are working on | Typical number of sessions |
|---|---|
| Stopping smoking | One intensive session |
| A specific phobia (needles, flying, spiders) | One to three |
| Pre-surgery anxiety | Two to three, depending on your date |
| Anxiety and stress | Three to six |
| Weight and eating patterns | Four to six |
| Confidence and self-esteem | Three to six |
| Long-standing or complex patterns | Discussed honestly at the outset |

These are ranges, not promises. Anyone who gives you an exact number before meeting you is guessing, and anyone who guarantees an outcome is making a claim they cannot support.

## Three Things That Move the Number

**How long the pattern has been running.** Something that started two years ago generally shifts faster than something that has been part of your life since childhood. Not always, but usually.

**Whether it is one thing or several tangled together.** "I want to stop smoking" is one goal. "I want to stop smoking, but I smoke when I am stressed, and I am stressed because of work" is three, and they need unpicking in order.

**What you do between sessions.** Clients who listen to the audio they are given, and practise what they have been shown, consistently need fewer appointments. This is the biggest variable of the three, and the only one entirely within your control.
