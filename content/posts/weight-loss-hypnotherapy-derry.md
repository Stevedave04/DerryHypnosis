## The Diet Industry's Dirty Secret

The diet industry is worth billions — and it depends on you failing.

Research consistently shows that 95% of people who lose weight through dieting will regain it within five years, often ending up heavier than before. Yet we blame ourselves, assume we lack discipline, and buy the next programme.

The truth is simpler and more uncomfortable: *diets don't work* because they address what you eat, not *why* you eat.

## Eating Is Rarely About Hunger

Think about the last time you reached for food when you weren't truly hungry. Perhaps it was stress at work. Loneliness. Boredom. Celebration. The desire to self-soothe after a difficult day.

These patterns are written into the subconscious mind, often in childhood. Food becomes associated with comfort, reward, punishment, love, or control — and those associations run far deeper than any meal plan can reach.

This is why someone can know exactly what they should eat, understand the calorie content of every item in the fridge, and still find themselves standing at the kitchen counter at 11pm eating cereal from the box. The conscious mind has the information. The subconscious mind has the power.

## How Hypnotherapy Addresses the Root Cause

Clinical hypnotherapy works at the level where eating patterns actually live — in the subconscious mind.

In a relaxed hypnotic state, Tracey is able to:

- **Identify the emotional drivers** behind your eating — the specific feelings, memories, and situations that trigger comfort eating, binge episodes, or cravings for sugar and processed food
- **Reframe the relationship between food and emotion** — so that food returns to its natural role as fuel, rather than as therapy, reward, or punishment
- **Install a 'Virtual Gastric Band' effect** — a powerful suggestion that helps your brain register fullness more quickly and accurately, reducing portion sizes naturally without any surgical procedure
- **Build a new body identity** — helping you feel, think, and act like the person you want to become, rather than constantly fighting against who you believe yourself to be

The difference from dieting is profound. Instead of white-knuckling your way through cravings, the cravings simply lose their hold.

## What a Typical Session Looks Like

Your first appointment begins with a detailed conversation. Tracey will ask about your history with food and weight, your triggers, your lifestyle, and what you've tried before. This isn't therapy — it's intelligence gathering, so that the hypnotherapy session itself is precisely tailored to you.

The hypnotherapy portion of the session lasts around 45–60 minutes. You will be guided into a state of deep, comfortable relaxation — similar to the drowsy feeling just before sleep. In this state, your subconscious is highly receptive to new suggestions and perspectives.

Most people find the experience profoundly peaceful. Many say it's the most relaxed they've felt in years.

After the session, many clients notice changes that feel almost effortless:

- Natural reduction in portion sizes
- Reduced cravings for sugar and high-fat foods
- Increased motivation to move and be active
- A calmer, more neutral relationship with food
- Improved self-image and body confidence

## How Many Sessions Will You Need?

This depends on the individual. Some clients see significant change after two or three sessions. Others benefit from a longer programme of four to six sessions, particularly where the emotional relationship with food is complex or longstanding.

Tracey will discuss a realistic plan during your first consultation and will always be honest about what's achievable and how quickly.

## What Hypnotherapy Is Not

It's worth being clear about what clinical hypnotherapy cannot do:

- It cannot make you lose weight without any effort on your part
- It does not put you to sleep or make you unconscious
- It cannot make you do anything you don't genuinely want to do
- It is not a substitute for medical advice regarding conditions like diabetes or metabolic disorders

What it *can* do is remove the subconscious obstacles that have made sustained weight loss so difficult in the past — and make the changes you want to make feel natural, rather than like constant deprivation.

The most important factor in the success of weight loss hypnotherapy is genuine motivation. You need to want to change — not because someone else thinks you should, not because of shame or social pressure, but because you genuinely want a healthier, freer life.

If that desire is there, hypnotherapy can help you align your entire mind — conscious and subconscious — behind it.
