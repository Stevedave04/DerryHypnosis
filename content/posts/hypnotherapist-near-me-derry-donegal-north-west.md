## When Online Is the Better Choice

Online sessions run by secure video, and for a lot of people they are not a compromise at all.

They tend to suit you if:

- You live an hour or more away, and the work needs several sessions
- You are in Donegal or further into the Republic and would rather not make the trip repeatedly
- You have childcare, shift work or mobility to work around
- You are recovering from surgery or illness and travelling is the last thing you need
- You would genuinely relax more deeply at home than in an unfamiliar room

They suit you less if home is noisy, you share a small space with other people, or your internet is unreliable. Hypnotherapy needs an uninterrupted hour in a room where you will not be disturbed. If you cannot guarantee that, travelling in is the better option.

One thing that surprises people: being at home does not weaken the effect. You are not being "put under" by proximity. You are being guided into a state you are perfectly capable of reaching in your own armchair.

## How to Check Anyone You Are Considering

This applies whether you choose Derry Hypnosis or anyone else, and it matters more than distance.

**Hypnotherapy is not a legally protected title** in the UK or Ireland. Anyone can call themselves a hypnotherapist. That is not a reason to avoid the profession, but it is a reason to check.

Before you book with anyone, ask:

- **Which body are you accredited by?** Look for a recognised professional organisation. Tracey is accredited by the European Association of Professional Hypnotherapists (EAPH).
- **Are you insured?** Any legitimate practitioner carries professional indemnity insurance and will say so without hesitation.
- **What is your training?** A weekend certificate and a clinical diploma are not the same thing.
- **What happens if this is not the right approach for me?** A good answer involves referring you elsewhere. Be wary of anyone who says hypnotherapy fixes everything.
- **Are you vetted to work with young people?** Relevant if the appointment is for a teenager.

Anyone who bristles at those questions has told you something useful.
