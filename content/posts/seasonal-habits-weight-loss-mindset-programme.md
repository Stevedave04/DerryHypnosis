The evenings get darker. The weather turns colder. We spend more time indoors and the cosy nights begin. Before you notice it happening, familiar patterns start running again.

*"I'll start again Monday."*

*"I've been good all day, so I deserve this."*

*"I'm having a bad week."*

*"I'll get back on track after Christmas."*

Sound familiar?

## Knowing What to Eat Is Rarely the Difficult Part

Most people already know which foods support their health. You have read the articles. You have followed the plans. You have counted, tracked, weighed and restricted.

The harder part sits underneath the food. It sits in the patterns, emotions, habits and automatic responses running in the background for years. The reach for the biscuit tin at nine o'clock. The reward after a difficult day at work. The internal rule saying the week is already ruined, so you might as well finish the packet.

Willpower asks you to fight those responses. Clinical hypnotherapy takes a different approach and works with the part of the mind where the responses were formed.

## An 8-Week Clinical Hypnotherapy Mindset and Habits Programme

This is not a traditional weight-loss class. There is no weekly weigh-in, no calorie sheet and no meal plan.

I am running an eight-week programme for a small group of seven to ten people, focused on the psychology behind long-term change. Across the eight weeks we work on:

- Changing limiting beliefs
- Understanding and managing cravings
- Exploring emotional eating
- Breaking old patterns
- Building sustainable habits
- Developing a healthier relationship with food
- Creating a future identity to support your goals
- Maintaining progress when life gets difficult

The aim is to help you understand why you do what you do, then develop the ability to respond differently.
