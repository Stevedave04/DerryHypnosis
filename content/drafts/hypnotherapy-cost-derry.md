## How Many Sessions Will You Actually Need?

This is the question behind the question. A single session fee means very little if you have no idea whether you are buying one or ten.

Honest ranges, based on how this work typically goes:

These are ranges, not promises. Anyone who guarantees an exact number before meeting you is guessing, and anyone who guarantees an outcome is making a claim they cannot support.

## What Moves the Number Up or Down

Three things make more difference than anything else.

**How long the pattern has been running.** Something that started two years ago generally shifts faster than something that has been part of your life since childhood. Not always — but usually.

**Whether it is one thing or several tangled together.** "I want to stop smoking" is one goal. "I want to stop smoking, but I smoke when I am anxious, and I am anxious because of work" is three, and they need unpicking in order.

**What you do between sessions.** Clients who listen to the audio they are given, and practise what they have been shown, consistently need fewer appointments. This is the single biggest variable, and it is the one entirely within your control.

## Is It Worth It?

Reasonable question, and the answer depends on what you are comparing it to.

If you smoke twenty a day, you are spending well over £4,000 a year. A single session costs a fraction of one month of that.

If the issue is anxiety that has you turning down work, cancelling plans or losing sleep, the cost is harder to put a number on but it is not nothing. Most people can name what it has already cost them.
